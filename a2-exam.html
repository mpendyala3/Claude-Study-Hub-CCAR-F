60 questions · 120 minutes · pass at 720

Written to the 37 Attempt-2 test objectives and shaped like the exam you actually sat: long scenarios, and every item spanning two or three objectives at once, so no question can be answered from a single remembered fact. Six systems recur throughout — an automated pull-request reviewer, a bank's support orchestrator, a parallel research pipeline, a claims extraction pipeline, a 400-engineer Claude Code rollout, and a multi-tenant MCP service desk. Every question explains the rule behind the right answer and why each distractor fails.

Read the objective tags Each item is labelled with the objectives it draws on (for example O7 · O10). When you get one wrong, that tag tells you which section of the CCAR-A2 documentation to go back to.

Study mode recommended first

Check each answer as you go and read the explanation immediately, while the question is still in your head. No time pressure. This is the mode that teaches.

Exam mode simulation

120-minute countdown, no feedback until you submit — the same conditions as the real thing. Use it once you have worked through study mode, to find out what survives time pressure.

Before you start

  • Select-2 items. 9 questions ask for exactly two answers. They are marked. Both must be right to score — and the two are usually complementary layers, not two phrasings of the same idea. If your picks are near duplicates, one is wrong.
  • Scenarios repeat. Six systems carry all 60 items. Read each one carefully the first time; after that you can skim it.
  • Items are deliberately long. Read the last sentence of the stem first to find out what is actually being asked, then go back for the two or three facts that bear on it.
  • No length tell. The correct option is never the longest and never the shortest, and the answer key is balanced across A/B/C/D. Guessing strategies score at chance here.
  • Scoring. Scaled 100–1000 like the real exam. 720 passes, which is about 42 of 60. Aim for 850+ here before you book.
  • Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the tab mid-attempt and come back. In exam mode the clock keeps running.
  • Keyboard: 1–4 to select, ←→ to move, F to flag, Enter to check in study mode.
