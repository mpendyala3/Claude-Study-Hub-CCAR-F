How to work a track Read the Docs, then do every Exercise, then sit the Mock exam in Exam mode — 120 minutes, no feedback until you submit. When you get a question wrong, its topic tag is the exact section of the Docs to go back to. Both mocks are deliberately harder than the live paper, so aim for 850+ before you book.
