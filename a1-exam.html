Scenario-based items weighted to the Attempt-1 blueprint, set inside six realistic businesses: a retail bank's support agent, a marketplace's multi-agent order operations, an SRE incident agent, an IT service desk, an insurance extraction pipeline, and a 400-engineer Claude Code rollout. Every question explains the rule behind the right answer and why each distractor fails.

This bank was rewritten to be hard Every item was replaced. Distractors are now near-misses — options that are partly correct, or correct about something other than what was asked, or that fix the symptom one layer away from the cause. A third of the items are select-two. Several require tracing code or reading a measurement rather than recalling a rule. If you were scoring comfortably on the old bank, expect this one to hurt; that is the point.

Study mode recommended first

Check each answer as you go and read the explanation immediately, while the question is still in your head. No time pressure. This is the mode that teaches.

Exam mode simulation

120-minute countdown, no feedback until you submit — the same conditions as the real thing. Use it once you have worked through study mode, to find out what survives time pressure.

Before you start

  • Select-2 items. 10 questions ask for exactly two answers. They are marked. Both must be right to score — and the two are usually complementary layers, not two phrasings of the same idea. If your picks are near duplicates, one is wrong.
  • Scenarios repeat. Several questions share a business context. Read the scenario carefully the first time; after that you can skim it.
  • Scoring. Scaled 100–1000 like the real exam. 720 passes, which is about 42 of 60. On this rewritten bank, 780+ is a genuinely strong result.
  • No length tell. The correct option is never the longest and never the shortest, and the answer key is balanced across A/B/C/D. Guessing strategies score at chance.
  • Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the tab mid-attempt and come back. In exam mode the clock keeps running.
  • Keyboard: 1–4 to select, ←→ to move, F to flag, Enter to check in study mode.
